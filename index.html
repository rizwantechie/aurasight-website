AuraSight Eye & Wellness Centre is a premium destination for comprehensive eye care, vision rehabilitation, and holistic wellness in Ludhiana. We offer advanced eye examinations, specialty contact lens services, myopia control, vision therapy, Neuro-vision rehabilitation, Low Vision rehab, Ocular Prosthesis and optical dispensing with premium eyewear. Our Wellness Clinic offers holistic consultation for infertility, cupping therapy (Hijama), PCOD, PCOM, GIT problems, and personalized natural healthcare. AuraSight combines modern technology, evidence-based care, and personalized attention to deliver the highest standards of patient care for individuals of all ages.
